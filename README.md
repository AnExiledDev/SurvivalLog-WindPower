# Wind Power

Adds a Large Wind Turbine to Survival Log. Craft the Large Wind Turbine Package from 6 Scrap Metal, 4 Electrical Wire, 4 Stainless Steel Plate and 3 Electronic Components, unpack it, and install it in a large furniture spot outdoors: in the yard outside or on the 2F terrace.

The turbine has its own model and icon. It makes power day and night, more the windier the weather is:

| Weather | Power in the yard |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy Smog | 0 |
| Clear, and the cold clear days | 5 to 6 |
| Cloudy | 10 |
| Rainy | 17 |
| Continuous Rain | 22 |
| Mild Cold | 26 |
| Heavy Rain | 36 |
| Moderate Cold | 38 |
| Severe Cold | 48 |

A turbine on the 2F terrace stands higher in the wind and makes a fifth more, up to 58.

The blades speed up and slow down smoothly with the wind and stand still in Heavy Smog. Every so often a gust comes through: the whole top swings round to meet it and the blades speed up, then both settle back. Hover over the turbine to see, at its base, what it makes now and how strong the wind is. The weather box on the screen shows the wind too, as a Wind Intensity line.

This is an early test build.

## Save safety

The game saves each turbine as a Large Solar Panel, and the mod turns it back into a turbine when the save loads. If you remove the mod, your turbines become Large Solar Panels and the save still loads. A turbine package that is still in your bag or in storage is lost when the mod is removed, so install it first.

## Requirements

- Survival Log 1.1.20151.
- BepInEx for Survival Log.

## Install

Copy `WindPower.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins`.
