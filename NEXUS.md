Name: Wind Power
Summary: Adds a Large Wind Turbine you can craft, unpack and install in a large furniture spot outdoors, in the yard outside or on the 2F terrace. This is an early test build: for now it looks like the Large Solar Panel and makes power the same way. Keep a backup of your save, since a save with a turbine in it may not load once the mod is removed.

Description (BBCode):
Wind Power adds a Large Wind Turbine to Survival Log.

[size=5]Main features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Craft it[/b]: the Large Wind Turbine Package is crafted like the Large Solar Panel Package, from the same materials.[/*]
[*][b]Outdoors only[/b]: it fits a large furniture spot in the yard outside or on the 2F terrace, and nowhere indoors.[/*]
[*][b]English and Chinese[/b]: every name and description comes in both.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Known issues[/size]
This is an early test build. The turbine still looks like the Large Solar Panel and makes power the way the solar panel does.

A save with a turbine in it, placed or still packed, will most likely not load once the mod is removed. Keep a backup of your save before you install one.

[size=5]Requirements[/size]
[list]
[*]Survival Log 1.1.20151. A game update can break it until the mod is updated.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/survivallog/mods/12]BepInEx for Survival Log[/url]. Install it first.[/*]
[/list]
